Hover a region

Point at any part of the page and its name, element and purpose will appear here.

Index of parts

Numbers match the labels on the page. Hover or focus one to light up the part it names.

Containers and leaves

Most parts nest inside other parts, which is the whole point of the faint inner rings — hover the button group and you can see it sitting inside the hero. Only one part is ever strongly outlined: the smallest one under the pointer.

Same look, different element

Nothing on this page proves what an element is by looking at it. A <button> and a <div> can be pixel-identical; the difference is what the browser, the keyboard and a screen reader can do with them.

Everything in here is inert

The controls in the mock are stand-ins rather than real inputs — the diagram is a picture of a page, so nothing inside it should be focusable. The index above is real, and works with the keyboard.
